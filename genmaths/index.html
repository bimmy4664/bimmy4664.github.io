Guide to VCE General Maths

General Maths is one of 4 VCE Mathematics subjects offered by VCAA (the others being Foundational Maths, Maths Methods and Specialist Maths). Here I will discuss everything you need to know about learning General Maths.

General Maths is split into 4 categories:

  1. Finance
  2. Statistics (Univariate and Bivariate)
  3. Matrices
  4. Networks

In these categories I will discuss both the fundamentals and in-depth concepts, as well as explain how these concepts can be used in real-world situations outside of school.
