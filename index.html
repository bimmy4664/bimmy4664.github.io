A New Beginning

My thoughts

So, it turns out Neocities banned my account or something and now my website there has been deleted. Oh well. So I decided to take refuge here. I do remember having a backup on there from like a couple years ago but I can't be bothered trying to find and reupload that. Besides, I want to take this site in a new direction.

So what I've done is that I have designed my website entirely around the use of Lynx (a text-based web browser). If you're viewing this on any other browser, it's probs gonna look like shit, and that's intentional. I'm aiming to make this site (and eventually my computing life) completely text and terminal-based. I'll try and make semi-regular posts on here about my experiences with that, as well as my experiences of just being able to surf the web without falling into the walled gardens of big tech sites.

I hope you all follow me on this journey and bookmark this site for future viewing. :3

Regards, Bimmy
